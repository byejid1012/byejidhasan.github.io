About

A scientist in progress.

An MSc Biotechnology graduate, background in Biotechnology, Biochemistry & Genetics. My academic experience and studies have given me a general idea of molecular biology, genetics, cell biology, biochemistry and biotechnology and also a little idea of bioinformatics and research methodology.

The systematic literature review and meta-analysis of evidence from clinical studies was the focus of my postgraduate research, investigating the comparative efficacy of Topiramate and Naltrexone for Alcohol Use Disorder. In addition to my academic studies, I also have some professional experience as a Biomedical Coder, where I had interacted with clinical documentation, ICD-10 coding and biomedical terms.

I am specifically seeking to become a better researcher in the area of molecular biology, genetics, bioinformatics and biomedical research and hope to eventually be able to make a significant, evidence-based contribution to science.
