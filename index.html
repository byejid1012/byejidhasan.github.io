MSc Thesis - Research Project University of Greenwich

Topiramate vs Naltrexone for Alcohol Use Disorder

For my MSc research project, I conducted a systematic literature review and comparative meta-analysis of Topiramate and Naltrexone for AUD. I screened and cleaned peer-reviewed studies, extracted effect sizes and standard errors, and used JASP for random-effects meta-analysis, heterogeneity assessment, forest and funnel plot analysis, and sensitivity testing.
